## About Me

I am an Electrical and Electronics Engineering graduate from **Vellore Institute of Technology, Chennai**, currently pursuing an **M.Sc. in Nanoelectronic Systems at Technische Universität Dresden**, specialising in **Semiconductor Technology**.

My journey into nanoelectronics is driven by a long-standing interest in understanding how semiconductor devices and technologies shape the world around us. I chose to pursue my master's at TU Dresden with a clear ambition: to move beyond designing circuits and develop a deeper understanding of the materials, devices and manufacturing processes that make modern semiconductor technologies possible.

My academic and research experience has given me a foundation in semiconductor chip implementation, standard-cell technology mapping, RTL-to-GDSII workflows and physical performance evaluation. Through projects and research involving Cadence Genus and Innovus across TSMC 180 nm and 90 nm technology nodes, open-source implementation flows and semiconductor package modelling, I have explored different stages of the semiconductor development ecosystem. My research contributions include a **published Indian patent application**, an additional patent application and a journal manuscript under review, and an accepted book chapter with CRC Press.

At TU Dresden, I am strengthening this foundation through studies in semiconductor technology, plasma technology and organic semiconductors. I am particularly motivated to deepen my understanding of **semiconductor devices, fabrication processes, materials engineering, process integration and advanced manufacturing**.

My long-term goal is to contribute to the semiconductor industry as a **Semiconductor Technology Engineer**, working at the intersection of device technology, process development and manufacturing. I aspire to keep learning, take on meaningful technical challenges and contribute to the technologies that will shape the next generation of semiconductor devices and systems.
