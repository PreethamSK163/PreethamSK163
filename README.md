I am an Electrical and Electronics Engineering graduate from VIT Chennai, currently pursuing an **M.Sc. in Nanoelectronic Systems at Technische Universität Dresden**, specializing in **Semiconductor Technology**. My technical background includes semiconductor chip implementation, RTL-to-GDSII workflows, physical design, and chip-level performance evaluation using Cadence Genus and Innovus across TSMC 180 nm and 90 nm technology nodes.
